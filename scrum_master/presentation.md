# What is a Scrum Master?

A Scrum Master helps the team work well together.

- Helps the team follow Scrum.
- Organizes short team meetings.
- Helps remove problems that slow the team down.

---

# Our project: crum_master

The team works in small steps called **sprints**.

1. Choose tasks for the sprint.
2. Work together on the tasks.
3. Review the work and plan what to improve.
